How to play

  • Move the yellow paddle with your pointer, arrow keys, or A and D.
  • Keep the ball in play and break every brick to win.
  • You start with X balls (depending on difficulty). Missing the paddle costs one ball.
  • Each brick is worth 10 points.
Helpful bonus
Collect a water can or blue water drop for bonus points.
Dirty water
Catch the brown droplet and all collected bonus points are wiped out.
